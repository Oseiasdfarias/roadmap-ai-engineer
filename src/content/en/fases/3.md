In the third phase you work as a junior developer, even if the role does not involve AI. The goal is to learn how a team works: reviewed code, tests, deployment and deadlines.

## Context

Small and mid-sized companies look for people who know frontend, backend and AI. Have a base in everything and pick one area to go deep in. This is also the phase to practice interviews and take-home tests and to finish the portfolio projects.

Timelines are indicative and vary a lot between people and markets.
