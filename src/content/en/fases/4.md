The fourth phase is the transition to AI. You take on the AI tasks at the company where you work, put the final project in production and apply for AI Engineer roles.

## Context

Internal experience counts as AI experience. When the company has no demand, the project in production, with known cost and evals, plays that role. Knowing how to tell the story of each project is part of the job.

Timelines are indicative and depend on opportunities you do not fully control.
