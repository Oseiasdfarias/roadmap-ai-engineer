In the second phase you look for an internship in tech or nearby areas, such as development, data, systems support and software automation, and study levels 2 and 3 in parallel.

## Context

What you are buying in this phase is team experience, reviewed code and deadlines, not salary. AI internships exist, but they are rare; a position in a neighboring area also builds your career, as long as you keep studying and publishing projects.

Use the level 3 projects in your résumé and interviews. Timelines are indicative.
