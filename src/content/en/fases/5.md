In the fifth phase the target is AI roles at mature companies in the field: banks, digital retail and multinationals. Fluent English and accumulated experience make the difference.

## Context

In these companies teams are more structured and AI problems happen at real scale. Interviews include LLM system design and are often in English. Many openings come through referrals, which is why your network counts.

This phase has no fixed timeline and no promised outcome.
