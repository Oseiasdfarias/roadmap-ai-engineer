The first phase is preparation. The focus is building the technical base of level 1, making your GitHub activity visible and starting to read in English.

## Context

Junior AI Engineer openings are rare. The most common path goes through an internship and software development before reaching AI, which is why this phase focuses on what counts at any entry point: coding well, versioning your work and showing what you can do.

Timelines are indicative. What matters is consistency: a little every day weighs more than a long, rare effort.
